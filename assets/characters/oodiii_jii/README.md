# OODIII JII - Character Assets & Art Guide

- **Character ID**: `0`
- **Fighter Name**: `OODIII JII`
- **Combat Style**: Heihachi Mishima + Raiden
- **Costume Theme**: Traditional Mishima black Karate Gi with a heavy gold obi belt and Heihachi swept-back wing horns.

To replace the procedural model with a custom Blender 3D model, export your rigged character as:
`assets/characters/oodiii_jii/model.glb`
DarkLotus will automatically load and animate it in 3D!
